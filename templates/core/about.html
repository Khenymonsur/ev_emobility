About CloudRide

Silent streets. Cleaner movement. Stronger cities.

We are building electric mobility services that help people, companies, and cities move with less noise, less fuel dependency, and more confidence.

Our mission

Electrifying everyday travel, one reliable trip at a time.

CloudRide connects customers to electric rides, rentals, charging access, and practical fleet support. We focus on dependable service first, then grow the technology around the real needs of riders, drivers, businesses, and charging partners.

Our purpose is to make clean mobility feel normal: easy to book, comfortable to use, affordable to adopt, and credible enough for organizations planning a lower-emission transport future.

Mission

Make electric mobility accessible and reliable for African city life.

Vision

A connected ecosystem where clean energy, mobility, and technology support better urban movement.

Values

Safety, environmental leadership, practical innovation, and service customers can trust.

Who we serve

Built for riders, drivers, businesses, and cities.

Riders

Comfortable electric trips for daily movement, appointments, and scheduled plans.

Operators

Tools and charging support that make EV operations easier to manage.

Organizations

Employee transport, fleet transition support, and cleaner corporate mobility.
